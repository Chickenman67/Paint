## ERRORS FIXED (all six verified against Wikipedia / primary sources)

- **E1** Clause 12 said "Vela." PSR B1257+12 is in **Virgo**; Vela hosts PSR B0833-45, a different pulsar with no planets. The constellation word is gone; the designation stays.
- **E2** Clause 9 said "about a sixth of a second." True spin period is 6.2185 ms (160.8 rev/s) — the old line was 27x wrong. Now: "six thousandths of a second."
- **E3** Clause 14 said two of the three weigh about as much as the Moon. True masses are Draugr 0.020, Phobetor 3.9, Poltergeist 4.3 Earth masses — ONE is Moon-ish and TWO are about four Earths, the exact inverse of the old shape.
- **E4** Clause 16 said 66 / 204 / 365 days. True periods are 25.262, 66.5419 and 98.2114 days.
- **E5** Clauses 19-20 tied a 1992 wobble to a 1992 discovery and then added two more years. Pulsar discovered 9 Feb 1990; first two planets announced Jan 1992, third later in 1992. Clause 19 now states the 1990 discovery; the unsourced "nobody believed him" is dropped rather than reworded; clause 20’s two-year wait is retained and is now the 1990->1992 gap.
- **E6** Clauses 28-29 asserted a base-eight joke by Wolszczan and Frail. No such joke exists — the octal reading of 1257 is a numerical accident. Removed and replaced with the verifiable fact: PSR 1257+12 is a sexagesimal sky coordinate, 12h57m right ascension and plus 12 degrees declination.
