SINGLE BIGGEST GAP, named by the critic: our frames are flat vector and
under-filled. Five of six. The win (q01) is the only one of the six with
real pigment in the fills AND a subject that dominates the frame AND legible

Note what this is NOT. It is not a timing problem and not a layout problem.
The v2 rebuild fixed cadence and motion, which was the user's complaint, and
those are not what the critic is scoring. The critic is scoring the surface,
and the surface is still clean digital vector.

path is not missing from the codebase -- it is missing from SOME CHAPTERS.
Texture coverage is inconsistent, not absent. Measure which chapters call the
paint primitives before changing anything.

=== ROUND 3 OPENING: WHAT THE PIGMENT GATE ACTUALLY MEASURED ===

Built lib/_pigment_gate.py (renders, never source-scans) and calibrated it on six
frames the critic ruled on, all inspected at 1280x720. The metric agreed with the
eye on all six and split them cleanly:

    painted  fortknox 75.82 =34.27  svalbard 29.04 =42.83  vatican 27.62 =23.56
    FLAT     fortknox 32.49 = 2.49  svalbard 67.75 = 3.20  vatican 64.44 = 3.19

Per-BEAT sweep (335 beats) -> 265 flat, all nine chapters. Trimodal:
75 below 3, 172 in 3-5, 18 in 5-8, 60 at 8+. The >=8 painted cluster is almost
entirely fortknox b26-b33/b36/b41 (gold brick vault) and svalbard b08/b10 (snow) --
the two frames I confirmed painted by eye. Everything else is smooth.

THEN THE MECHANISM TURNED OUT TO BE NOT WHAT I ASSUMED. I expected "the flat beats
don't call the paint path." Wrong. mezhgorye (~60 PA.fill_* calls) and svalbard
(~40) both paint heavily and still score flat. Root cause, read off v2paint.fill_poly:

    tooth_w = VALUE_FINE + DARK * (1.22 - luma/255)

On a cream wall (luma ~210) that is ~1.9 levels -- invisible. The broad VALUE drift
(cells=5) is smooth, so a light fill reads as a soft gradient. The paint system
deliberately kills tooth on light colours, and this film is mostly light colours.

I tried to fix it in the engine (raised light-fill tooth, bristle streaks) on a
calibration sheet and it BARELY MOVED (tile 3.76 -> 4.75) -- all four treatments read
as out-of-focus blur, none looked like paint. Two reasons that was wasted effort,
both now recorded in memory pigment-metric-conflates-edge-density-with-texture:

  1. A per-tile luma-std "pigment" metric measures EDGE DENSITY, not paint. A frame
     of ~40 small outlined slabs scores 53; a single big flat fill never passes ~5
     no matter how much noise you add. The metric was rewarding COMPOSITION.
  2. The real reference surface is a fine woven CANVAS TOOTH (~2-4px) plus broad
     soft drift -- see q06_A, the ref book. Not high-frequency noise, not clouds.

SO THE GOVERNING GAP IS COMPOSITION + FINE TOOTH, NOT A PAINT-ENGINE REWRITE:
  - the reference CROPS its subject at the frame edges and fills the composition
    (q06 book fills the frame; our losses were the under-filled ones)
  - our "flat" frames are big smooth shapes + dead space + no character
    (vatican book, svalbard "2016" chart)
  - surface needs a fine ~2-4px canvas grain, deliberately subtle

Next round: per-chapter composition (frame-fill, edge-rich subjects, character
presence), fanned out to builders, judged by me at ship size. Light global canvas
tooth only. Do NOT rewrite the paint engine constants again -- round 3 already
tried that and the critic still read 9/10 as flat.


================================================================================
CANVAS TOOTH: MEASURED AND REJECTED (2026-10-07)
================================================================================
The note above said "Light global canvas tooth only." That advice is now DISPROVED
by measurement. I do not want a future round following it.

Test: work3/lib/_calib_tooth.py -- woven crosshatch (warp+weft, 3px period) applied
to three REAL, eye-confirmed-flat frames at ship size (1280x720), three amplitudes.
judge-texture-at-ship-size applied: no synthetic swatch this time.

    frame            amp0    amp4    amp8
    pinegap   b10     3.03    3.59    4.89
    vatican   b18     3.44    3.96    5.16
    mezhgorye b15     5.42    5.78    6.63
                        (PAINT_STD bar is 8.0)

Two failures, and they compound:
  1. METRIC. Best case is 6.63 -- still under the 8.0 bar. A global surface pass
     cannot reach the painted cluster, because the metric is counting EDGES and a
     uniform overlay adds ~1.5 levels of variance, not shape.
  2. EYE. At amp=4 nothing is visible. At amp=8 the vatican cream pages show a
     clear CROSSHATCH GRID -- it reads as a screen-door / digital dither artifact,
     which is worse than the smooth gradient it replaced. v2paint's own header
     warns: per-pixel uniform noise "is the one texture that always reads as
     digital dither / video noise rather than as paper."

CONCLUSION. Surface treatment is a dead lever here. This is the THIRD independent
confirmation (synthetic bristle swatch 3.76->4.75; real-frame canvas tooth
3.03->6.63; both by eye invisible-to-dithery). The lever is COMPOSITION ONLY:
frame-fill + edge-rich subjects + character presence. That is what the builders
are doing. Do not spend another round on texture.
